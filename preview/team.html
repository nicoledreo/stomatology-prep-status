Template composition. Nothing on this page is a fact about a real practice.

Every photograph here is a generated template image. So are the practice name, the town, the street address, and all three staff names with their roles and the lines beside them. None of it describes a real practice, and all of it has to be replaced before ship. Each slot carries a swap marker naming what replaces it.

THE REGISTER ENTRIES ARE AN OPEN OWNER BLOCKER AND THIS IS THE PAGE THEY BLOCK. Every card below reserves a clinical title, a register name, a registration number and a verification line, and renders all four EMPTY. That is by design and it is not a rendering fault: a template credential is a fabricated qualification and an invented registration number is the one invention with legal teeth. What the owner owes, one item per empty slot: real names, the register named, the registration number for each, the title string exactly as that register prints it, and the name and date of whoever verified each entry. Nothing else releases them.

A SECOND THING IS FLAGGED FOR THE OWNER RATHER THAN FOR THE SWAPPER, and the brief calls it the largest finish gap on the site. The navigation label, the browser title and the heading of this page all say who you will see, and the three people on it are a Practice Manager, a Treatment Coordinator and a Reception Lead. No clinician appears, because a name beside a clinical role implies a licence nobody has supplied. The page does not apologise for that in its own copy and it is not meant to: the standfirst under the heading is the string that scopes what this page covers, and it is the string to re-read on the day real clinical profiles arrive.

This is the team page body on its own. There is no site header, no footer and no closing call to action, because the chrome owns all three and has not been built yet. The links below point at sibling pages that are not all built yet either. The four route labels and both routes to the questions page are working identifiers the owner may replace, not approved copy. The brief declares three photograph slots for this page and this composition renders exactly three, so unlike the homepage and the services page there is no image-slot reconciliation owed here.
