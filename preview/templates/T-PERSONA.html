THE REGISTER ENTRIES ARE AN OPEN OWNER BLOCKER AND THIS IS THE PAGE THEY BLOCK. Every card below reserves a clinical title, a register name, a registration number and a verification line, and renders all four EMPTY. That is by design and it is not a rendering fault: a template credential is a fabricated qualification and an invented registration number is the one invention with legal teeth. Nothing releases those four slots except real values from the owner.

The practice name, the town, the street address, the telephone number, the email address, the opening hours and all three staff names with their roles and lines are template values standing in for information the owner has not supplied. Each one carries a swap marker naming what replaces it. The three photographs are generated template images, not this practice's rooms.

NO CLINICIAN APPEARS ON THIS PAGE AND THAT IS DELIBERATE. The three people are a Practice Manager, a Treatment Coordinator and a Reception Lead, three roles that hold no register entry in any candidate regime. A name beside a clinical role would imply a licence nobody has supplied, so no card carries a title of any kind and no picture on this page is a face.

THE PROOF SLOT IS HELD. The type registry asks this page for at least one persona-proof element. Proof is omitted on this build, never templated and specifically never shipped behind a representative-example caption, so the floor is UNMET and the slot at section five carries a plain factual record instead. The reasoning sits in the section's own ledger comment rather than only here.
