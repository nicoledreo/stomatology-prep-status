About Alder Grove Dental

A dental practice in Riverbend, and how it works.

How a visit here works

Most visits here follow the same few steps.

You come in at reception and give your details. The first time there's a bit more to take down than there will be afterwards.

You wait in the waiting area until it's your turn. Then you go through to the treatment room, where the examination happens.

Afterwards you hear what the examination turned up, in plain words. You take it away in writing, so you can read it again later.

If there is work to do, nothing is agreed until you have heard what it involves. We book it as a separate appointment rather than starting it on the spot.

Why it runs in that order

The order is deliberate.

Looking at something, explaining it, and deciding what to do about it are three separate steps, and we keep them separate. The examination is its own appointment. What it turned up goes in writing, so you have it. Anything that follows is booked afterwards rather than started on the day.

The gap is on purpose. It is your time to think it over. Ask anything you still want to ask before you decide.

What we keep to

You hear what something involves before it happens, and you hear it in plain words.

Whatever the examination turns up goes in writing, so you can read it again after the appointment.

Every part of the practice has one person whose job it is, so you know who to ask.

Who you'll see

The people who look after reception, appointments and the day-to-day running of the practice.
