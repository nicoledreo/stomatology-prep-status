# Preview

Six page bodies from the Alder Grove Dental build, as they currently render.

**Open `index.html`.** The links between pages work, so you can move around the site.

## Read these first, or you will misread the pages

**Nothing here is a fact about a real practice.** "Alder Grove Dental", the town of Riverbend, the
street address, the telephone number, the email address, the opening hours, the three staff names and
the four service names are all **placeholders**. The telephone sits in the range reserved for fiction
and the email uses the reserved `.example` domain, so neither reaches anybody. Every one of those
values carries a swap marker naming what replaces it. Each page says so on its own face, in the strip
at the top.

**Every photograph is generated.** None of them shows this practice, its rooms, its equipment or its
people. They were generated to a written brief and audited before use, and they are placeholders
exactly like the text is.

**There is no chrome.** No site header, no footer, no navigation bar and no closing call to action.
Those are drawn once, against finished page bodies, rather than guessed at before the bodies exist.
That is why there is no menu at the top of any page, and it is deliberate rather than unfinished.

**These pages have not been adversarially verified.** That pass is batched to the end, at the owner's
request, because revisions are coming. One check was not deferred: a consistency sweep across all six,
diffing computed type scale, spacing, button geometry, card radius, shadows, gradient stops and
animation curves. Its findings are in section 15 of the main README.

## What the build refuses to do, and why that shows

There are no testimonials, no star ratings, no review counts, no awards, no accreditation badges and
no statistics anywhere on these pages, **not even as captioned placeholder examples**. There are no
photographs of people, so the team page uses designed monogram avatars rather than faces. There is no
guarantee and no risk-reversal line. Nothing claims anything about parking, entrances, floors or
travel time.

None of that is an oversight. A site built from a theme demo has no first-party truth to draw on, so
anything that looks like proof would have to be invented, and an invented proof is a false one whatever
caption sits under it. Where the build needed a fact nobody had confirmed, it says so on the page
instead of filling the gap.
