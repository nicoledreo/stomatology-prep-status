About Alder Grove Dental

A dental practice in Riverbend, and how it works.

How a visit here works

A visit here runs the same way each time. You come in at reception and give your details (the first time there is a little more to take down than there will be afterwards). You wait in the waiting area until you are called through, and the examination itself happens in the treatment room. We look, we say what we have found in plain words, and we write it down for you. If there is work to do, we set out what it involves before anything is agreed, and it is booked as a separate appointment rather than started on the spot.

Why it runs in that order

The order is deliberate. Looking at something, explaining it, and deciding what to do about it are three separate steps, and we keep them separate: the examination is its own appointment, what we found is written down, and anything that follows is booked afterwards rather than started on the day. The gap between finding something and acting on it is part of the arrangement, not a delay in it.

What we keep to

  • We say what something involves before we do it.

  • We write down what we found, so it can be read again after the appointment.

  • Every part of the practice has one person whose job it is, so you know who to ask.
